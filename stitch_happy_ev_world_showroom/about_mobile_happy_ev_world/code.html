Navigating Kolkata demands a different kind of mobility. We saw a gap between the promise of electric vehicles and the reality of experiencing them locally. Happy EV World was established to bridge that gap.

We aren't just selling scooters; we are curating a transition to cleaner, smarter urban transport right here in Sodepur. Our showroom is designed to be a transparent, pressure-free environment where you can explore the technology at your own pace.
