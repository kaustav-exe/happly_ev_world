ABOUT HAPPY EV WORLD

Electric Mobility,
Closer To Home.

A premium electric vehicle destination in Sodepur, Kolkata.

Built For The Way
Our City Moves.

Happy EV World was founded with a singular vision: to bridge the gap in local, sustainable mobility in Sodepur and the greater Kolkata area. We recognized that the transition to electric vehicles shouldn't be a distant, inaccessible concept, but a tangible reality for our community.

By bringing a curated selection of premium electric scooters directly to Sodepur, we're not just selling vehicles; we're facilitating a shift towards cleaner, quieter, and more efficient urban transportation. Our focus is on providing an unparalleled showroom experience that respects the sophisticated engineering of the vehicles we represent.
