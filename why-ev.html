WHY ELECTRIC

A Better Way To Move

Less noise. Less everyday running cost. A smarter, cleaner way to experience your city commutes.

Electric Changes The Everyday.

Zero Emissions

Breathe cleaner air in Kolkata. Electric drivetrains produce zero tailpipe emissions, helping build a healthier urban community.

Instant Torque

Experience immediate, seamless acceleration. The motor delivers instantaneous power for responsive overtaking and confidence.

Minimal Maintenance

No engine oil, spark plugs, or complex mechanical gears. Enjoy a simplified ownership experience with up to 70% lower upkeep costs.
